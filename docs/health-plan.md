# Your Hashan Fit health plan

Hello Hashan. This is your whole plan in one place.

It starts on **Sunday 8 November 2026**. All times are Riyadh time.

The idea is simple. Eat a little less. Eat more protein. Lift weights five days a week. Walk more. Sleep a little earlier. Do small things every day, and the results will come.

The Hashan Fit app follows this plan for you. You can also read it in the app, in the **Plan** tab.

### How much energy your body uses

- At full rest, your body burns about **1,840 kcal a day**. This is your resting burn (BMR).
- With your desk job, 5 gym sessions a week and 10,000 steps a day, you burn about **2,800 kcal a day**.
- This number goes down slowly as your weight goes down.

You will eat 1,800 kcal a day. Your body burns more than that. It takes the difference from your stored fat. That is how you lose weight.

- **78 kg** later, around mid-April 2027.

- It is not too fast. You can still train well and keep your strength.

In Ramadan the loss is slower. You eat a little more and train a little less. This is part of the plan. It is fine.

**Water.** Drink 3 to 3.5 L a day. In the app, tap **+250 ml** each time you drink 250 ml.

### Steps

Build up your steps slowly.

| Week | Steps a day |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | 7,000 |
| Week 2 | 8,000 |
| Week 3 | 9,000 |
| Week 4 and after | 10,000 |

Your Fitbit Air counts your steps, and the app reads them. You can also type them in by hand. The 10-minute walk after lunch helps a lot.

### How to weigh yourself

Weigh yourself once a week, on **Friday morning**. Your schedule puts it at 8:00 AM.

1. Go to the toilet first.
2. Weigh before you eat or drink anything.
3. Use the same scale every time.
4. If you want, measure your waist at the navel (belly button) on the same day. This is optional.
5. Type the numbers into the Friday weigh-in card in the app.

Your weight changes from day to day because of water and food. One weigh-in a week, done the same way each time, gives you a clear picture.

### The two-week check

Every two weeks, the app checks your speed. It asks one question: "Am I losing at the right pace?"

- **When:** at the Friday weigh-in of weeks 4, 6, 8, 10 and 12.
- **Why it skips weeks 1 to 3:** in the first weeks you lose a lot of water. This makes the numbers look better than they really are. So the app waits.
- **How it works:** the app compares today's weight with your weight from two weeks ago. From that, it works out how much you lost per week.

Then it gives you a suggestion:

| Your weekly loss | What the app suggests |
|---|---|
| Below 0.8 kg | Lower calories by 150 kcal. If that would go under 1,600 kcal, add 2,000 steps instead |
| 0.8 to 1.5 kg | No change. Keep going |
| Above 1.5 kg | Raise calories by 150 kcal |

- The app only suggests. You choose **Accept** or **Ignore**.
- The check does not run during Ramadan.

## 7. Lipo 6 Ultra

The full name is Nutrex Lipo-6 Black Ultra Concentrate.

### What it is

- Each capsule has **200 mg of caffeine**.
- It also has synephrine, yohimbine and rauwolscine. The label does not say how much of each.
- The label says:
  - Do not take it within 6 hours of sleep.
  - Do not take it with other caffeine.
  - Use it for 2 months at most, then take a break.

### What the evidence says

- **Caffeine** gives a small fat-loss effect.
- **Synephrine** showed no effect on weight. It raised blood pressure by about 6/4 mmHg.
- The evidence for **yohimbine** is weak. It also carries heart-rhythm and blood-pressure risks.
- A 200 mg caffeine dose should be taken about 13 hours before bed, so it does not hurt your sleep.

So the capsule is not magic. Your food, training, steps and sleep do most of the work.

### Your rule: 1 capsule a day

Today you take 2 capsules a day. From 8 November, you take only 1.

- Take **1 capsule around 9:00 AM**, between meals.
- **Never after 10:00 AM.** No afternoon capsule.
- Take it for **8 weeks only**, from **8 November to 2 January**. Then stop.
- **None during Ramadan.**

The app helps with this. Before 10 AM it shows "Take 1 capsule (before 10 AM)". After 10 AM, if you have not taken it, it shows "Skip today".

### Check your blood pressure every week

- Before you start, measure your blood pressure and resting heart rate at a pharmacy.
- Then measure them again **every Friday**.
- The Friday weigh-in card in the app has optional fields for both.

### Warning signs: stop right away

Stop Lipo 6 immediately if you get any of these:

- Palpitations (a fast or strange heartbeat)
- Chest pain
- Dizziness
- A severe headache
- Shortness of breath
- Poor sleep

If any of these happen, see a doctor.

### Check that it is registered in Saudi Arabia

Check the product's SFDA registration in one of two ways:

- Open the SFDA **"Tameni"** app and scan the barcode on the bottle.
- Or call the SFDA hotline: **19999**.

If you are not sure about the result, ask a pharmacist or a doctor before you take more.

### Do not combine it with

- Pre-workout drinks
- Energy drinks
- Other fat burners
- Decongestants (pseudoephedrine, phenylephrine). These are in some cold and flu medicines.
- Antidepressants
